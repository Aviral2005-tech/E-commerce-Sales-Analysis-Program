# E-commerce Sales Analysis Report

## Objective

Analyze e-commerce sales data to identify sales trends, top-performing products, and regional performance.

## Analysis Performed

- Loaded sales data
- Removed duplicate records
- Handled missing values
- Calculated total sales
- Calculated average sales
- Identified highest sale
- Found best-selling product

## Visualizations

### 1. Sales by Product

Shows the revenue generated by each product.

### 2. Sales by Region

Shows regional contribution to total revenue.

## Insights

- Electronics contribute the highest revenue.
- Laptop is the best-selling product.
- North and South regions contribute the largest share of sales.
- Accessories have high sales volume but lower revenue than electronics.

## Conclusion

The analysis highlights sales performance across products and regions, helping businesses identify profitable products and market trends.
